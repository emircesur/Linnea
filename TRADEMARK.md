# License & Brand Guidelines

All source code in this repository is available under the [MIT License](LICENSE): you are free to use, modify, and
distribute it. However, this license applies strictly to the code and does not grant rights to the Linnea name or logo.

## What is permitted

- **Describing compatibility or origin:** You may state how your project relates to Linnea, for example "made with
  Linnea," "a Linnea film," "works with Linnea," "for the Linnea format," or "supports puppets and rich puppets formats
  for Linnea" (`.ppet` and `.rppet`).
- **Editorial and educational references:** You may mention Linnea in blog posts, articles, presentations, tutorials,
  courses, and videos.
- **Contributing upstream:** You may fork this repository and retain the original name while developing changes
  intended to be submitted back to the project.
- **Unmodified redistribution:** You may share exact, unaltered releases under their original name.

## What requires prior written permission

- **Rebranding derivatives and businesses:** Do not name a modified build, product, hosted service, or company
  "Linnea" (or anything confusingly similar). If you publish a fork as your own project, give it a distinct name.
- **Logo usage:** Do not use the Linnea logo, or any design that closely resembles it, for your own product or service.
- **Implied endorsement:** Do not suggest that the Linnea project created, sponsors, endorses, or officially supports
  your work if it does not.
- **Official-looking identifiers:** Do not include "linnea" in a domain name, package name, or account handle in a way
  that could appear official.
